Private vault

Passwords and other sensitive notes, locked with a passphrase only you know. No agent can see or change it: it is locked in this browser before it is stored, and it is kept apart from the agents. If you stop talking to Lorium for 7 days, Lorium sends it on WhatsApp to the person you choose.

Status

You count as around when you message Lorium on WhatsApp or use this console. Lorium warns you after 5 and 6 days of silence; reply anything to stop it.

Create your private vault

  1. Choose a passphrase: a few unrelated words is easy to remember and hard to guess (at least 12 characters).
  2. Give the passphrase to the person who should receive the vault, in person or on paper. Not on WhatsApp.
  3. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe. If you forget it, nobody can open the vault, not even LRMagent.

This passes on access, not your wishes: it does not replace a will or other legal documents.

Unlock to view or change

Unlocking happens in this browser. It locks again after 10 minutes without activity.

Who receives it

The backup person gets it at the same time, in case the first can't be reached. Give them the passphrase too.

A pause counts as you being around until that day; the 7 days start after it. The test sends you exactly what your person would get, with a link that works for 24 hours.

Passphrase and removal

After changing it, give the new passphrase to the person too.
