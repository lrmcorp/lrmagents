Gmail and Google CalendarRead and search mail, draft replies, send (after your OK), see and add events.
One-time setup (about 10 minutes)
  1. Open Google Cloud → New project, name it e.g. LRMagent, and create it.
  2. Turn on the two APIs: Gmail API and Google Calendar API → Enable on each.
  3. Open Google Auth Platform → Get started: app name LRMagent, your email, audience External. Under Audience, add your own Gmail address as a test user, then press Publish app (otherwise Google signs you out every 7 days). Google will show "unverified app" when you connect; that is expected for a private app: choose Advanced → Go to LRMagent.
